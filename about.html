About Me

My name is Divine-favour Bassey Eminue. I am from Akwa Ibom State and I am currently in my 5th year studying Computer Science at Akwa Ibom State University. When I am not coding or doing assignments, I like to watch movies, play football with my friends, and listen to music. I am a big fan of Afrobeats.

My favourite courses so far are Operating Systems, Computer Architecture, Data Communication, Database Management & Systems, and System Analysis. I like how they explain how computers work from the inside. I also enjoy web development, which is why I built this website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Currently, I am trying to learn more about web development and databases. I want to be able to build complete websites from scratch, not just the front-end. I am also interested in networking because I find how data moves across the internet really cool.

For my future career, I am not sure exactly what I want to do yet, but I know I want to work in tech. Maybe I will become a software developer or work in cybersecurity. I just want to keep learning and see where it takes me. For now, I am taking it one course at a time.
