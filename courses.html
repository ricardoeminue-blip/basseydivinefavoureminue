My Courses

These are some of the courses I have taken or am currently taking in my Computer Science programme. Some of them are more interesting than others, but I am trying to learn something from each one.

Course Name Description My Interest Level
Operating Systems How computer systems manage hardware, memory, and processes High
Computer Architecture How computers are designed internally and how they process data High
Data Communication How data is transmitted across networks, protocols, and internet infrastructure High
Database Management & Systems How to design and manage databases using SQL High
System Analysis How to study business problems and design computer-based solutions High

These are my favourite courses because they explain how computers really work from the inside. Operating Systems and Computer Architecture are interesting because I get to understand what happens when I press the power button. Data Communication is cool because I want to know how the internet actually works. Database Management is practical and I can use it to build real applications.
