Welcome to My Website

My name is Divine-favour Bassey Eminue and I am a 5th year Computer Science student at Akwa Ibom State University. I decided to create this website as part of my Net-Centric Computing assignment, but also because I think it is a good way to practise what I am learning in class.

I have always been interested in technology since secondary school. I used to go to cyber cafes just to browse and play games, and I always wondered how websites and apps are made. That curiosity is what made me choose Computer Science when I was applying for university.

Programming was not easy for me at first. When I started learning HTML, I was confused by all the tags and attributes. But with time and practice, things started making more sense. Now I am also learning Python and Java, and while they are harder, I am getting the hang of it slowly.

This website is simple, but I built it myself and I am proud of it. I know there is still a lot for me to learn, but I am enjoying the journey so far. Thanks for visiting my page.
