Welcome to RIC-EMI Stores

RIC-EMI Stores is your trusted destination for quality home appliances at affordable prices. We are a student-run online retail store that sells different types of appliances for your home, office, and personal use.

We started this store as part of our Net-Centric Computing assignment, but we want to show that it can be a real business idea. Whether you need a new fan for your room, a blender for the kitchen, or a pressing iron for your clothes, we have got you covered.

Our goal is to provide reliable products and good customer service. We understand that students and working people need affordable appliances that still work well, and that is exactly what we offer.

Feel free to check out our products page to see what we have in stock. You can also contact us if you have any questions or want to place an order.
