About RIC-EMI Stores

RIC-EMI Stores is an online retail store that was created as part of a Net-Centric Computing assignment. The name "RIC-EMI" is a combination of ideas from our team members, and we decided to use it as our brand name.

We are a team of Computer Science students at Akwa Ibom State University who wanted to create something practical and realistic. Instead of just writing code for an assignment, we thought it would be more interesting to build something that looks and works like a real online store.

Our store focuses on selling home appliances like fans, blenders, pressing irons, rice cookers, electric kettles, and lamps. These are things that students and families need every day, and we believe everyone should be able to afford them.

Although this is a school project, we have tried our best to make it look professional. The website is built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and it includes features like a product catalogue, shopping cart, and a contact form where customers can leave feedback.

RIC-EMI Stores may have started as an assignment, but we hope to develop it further in the future. Thank you for visiting our store.
